Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Shorts Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent it uses Google OAuth to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, to read that channel's video list, and to read that channel's YouTube Analytics reports.

What it stores

It stores the titles, upload status and performance figures of the videos it has uploaded, so that it can report on them. It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers or about anyone other than the channel owner.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
